# Pricing and Billing

## Plans

- **Basic** — EUR 49 per month, up to 3 worker nodes, 100 GB of managed storage.
- **Pro** — EUR 390 per month, up to 25 worker nodes, 2 TB of managed storage.
- **Enterprise** — custom pricing, starts at EUR 2,500 per month, unlimited nodes.

Prices exclude VAT. Customers in the EU with a valid VAT ID are invoiced with reverse charge.

## Annual billing

Paying annually upfront gives a **15% discount** on the Basic and Pro plans. Enterprise
discounts are negotiated in the contract.

## Overage

Storage above the plan limit is charged at EUR 0.09 per GB per month. Egress traffic
above 1 TB per month is charged at EUR 0.05 per GB. Overage is calculated daily and
billed with the next invoice.

## Invoices and payment terms

Invoices are issued on the 1st day of each month for the previous month's usage.
Payment terms are 14 days for Basic and Pro, and 30 days for Enterprise. Accepted
methods: card, SEPA direct debit, bank transfer (Enterprise only).

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, Orbitly retries the charge after 3 days. After a **7-day grace
period** the account is suspended: workloads keep running but new deployments are
blocked. After 30 days of suspension workloads are stopped.

## Refunds

Monthly plans are not refundable. Annual plans can be cancelled within the first
14 days for a full refund; after that a prorated refund is issued for unused full months.
