# Orbitly Customer Onboarding

## First steps

After the contract is signed, an onboarding manager holds a kickoff call
within 3 business days. During the call, the hosting region, the migration plan and
the customer's contact persons are agreed on.

## Data migration

Orbitly helps with data migration free of charge for volumes up to 500 GB. Migrations over 500 GB
are quoted separately. For Enterprise customers, the migration is performed by a dedicated engineer.

## Training

Pro plan customers get 2 free training sessions for their team, Enterprise customers get
5 free training sessions. Each session lasts 90 minutes and is held online
in Ukrainian or English.

## Dedicated manager

Enterprise customers get a dedicated manager (Technical Account Manager) who
holds quarterly architecture reviews and capacity planning sessions.
